Aetherion – Clash of Voices

A mystical strategy game where ancient magic, tactical combat, and voice-driven power collide.

"Dive into the chaos — where voices become power."

A World Shaped by Voices

In Aetherion, gods draw their power not from relics or weapons, but from spoken incantations. Ancient realms are torn apart by divine conflicts, where only those who master their voice can dominate the battlefield.

Voice-Driven Tactical Combat

Battles unfold in fast-paced 3v3 matches where spells are cast by voice instead of buttons. Every decision matters — timing, positioning, and teamwork define victory.

Handcrafted Pixel Art Visuals

Aetherion features predominantly hand-drawn pixel art, blending stylized arcane effects with carefully crafted visuals to keep battles readable, expressive, and immersive even at the height of chaos.

Meet the Team

Aetherion was crafted by five passionate students who live and breathe competitive MOBA battles. Fueled by coffee, late-night coding, and endless debates about ranks, they turned their love for strategy and magic into this chaotic voice-driven adventure.

Bug Hunter

Connection Overlord

Screen Sorcerer
